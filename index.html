About Alex

Alex Katana is an Evangelist and disciple maker from Msambweni, Ramisi, Kenya. He is passionate about sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ and helping people become disciples who make other disciples.

Alex enjoys Bible study, reading storybooks, meeting new people, and building meaningful friendships. He is committed to serving God and being available to go wherever God sends him.
